# Contractor Project Reliability Assessment

- **Status:** Active discovery / packaging module (electrical contractors)
- **Date:** 2026-11-09
- **Brief:** [../.aion/research/techops-monday-brief-2026-11-09.md](../.aion/research/techops-monday-brief-2026-11-09.md)
- **Security overlay:** [techops-secure-digital-contractor-infrastructure.md](techops-secure-digital-contractor-infrastructure.md)
- **Priority:** P1 — complements security; does not replace it

AI data-center financing and delivery news create **opportunity and execution
risk**. Design changes, supplier delays, and permitting uncertainty mean AION
should not bet the electrical contractor offer solely on announced megawatts.

Position around **operational reliability and margin protection**, with security
built into the underlying systems. Do not assume major data-center announcements
automatically create accessible contracts for smaller electrical businesses.

## SKU one-liner

> Assess whether estimating, procurement, change orders, documents, sub access,
> and schedule dependencies are reliable enough that digital systems (and later
> agents) protect margin instead of amplifying chaos.

## Assessment dimensions

| Dimension | What “good” looks like |
|---|---|
| Estimate → PO traceability | Quoted lines map to purchase orders without spreadsheet archaeology |
| Supplier lead times | Critical-path materials have dated lead times and alternates |
| Change-order approvals | Written approval before work/cost commits |
| Project-document versioning | Single current set of plans/specs; superseded docs marked |
| Subcontractor access | Time-bound identities; least privilege on files/systems |
| Schedule dependencies | Predecessor/successor visibility; delay owners named |

## Market-selection scorecard (strategy)

Use when choosing which contractors to pursue — independent of data-center hype:

| Factor | Prefer | Avoid / discount |
|---|---|---|
| Backlog quality | Signed work + deposits | Speculative chase-only backlog |
| Customer concentration | Diversified | Single mega-project dependence |
| Geographic exposure | Stable permitting climate | Heavy bet on contested data-center locales |
| Payment terms | Clear, enforceable | Chronic retainage / slow-pay chaos |
| Operational complexity | Documented processes | Heroics-only delivery |
| Digital maturity | Shared systems of record | Pure tribal knowledge |

## Packaging impact

| Offer | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Secure Digital Contractor Infrastructure** | Remain broadly applicable (docs, identity, backup, field devices, CRM) — not data-center-dependent |
| **This assessment** | Upsell / discovery module for margin protection + future Assist-tier ops agents |
| **Security** | EC1–EC10 controls stay mandatory for Ass/Execute recommendations |

## Explicit non-claims

- Financing announcements (e.g. hyperscaler-backed campuses) are not booked
  revenue for AION or for a given contractor.
- Local opposition / permitting risk can cancel or delay capacity; treat
  announced MW as uncertain demand.
- This module is not an OT/ICS cybersecurity assessment.
